## ⚠️ RED FLAGS TO AVOID
- Scholarships that require **paid membership** or an upfront fee
- Sites primarily used for **lead generation** (asking for excessive personal info)
- Vague awards with **many grammatical errors** on the site
- Programs that **guarantee** scholarships — legitimate scholarships are competitive

---

> **Tip:** Apply to scholarships year-round, not just in spring. Major awards open every single month. Applying to more = higher chance of winning. Start early and track every deadline.
